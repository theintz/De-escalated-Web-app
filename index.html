Non-Escalator Relationship Menu

This tool is a discussion aid for people in a relationship to identify and discuss their personal desires and relationship expectations. It's only the start of a conversation: revisit the menu when circumstances or relationships change.

  1. Fill out the menu. Skip anything you like, and add your own items if something is missing.
  2. Send your link to your partner. They fill out the same menu.
  3. You both see where your answers match.

Nothing is stored on a server. Your answers live only in your link and in this browser. Anyone who has your link can see your answers, so only share it with your partner.

Your partner has shared their answers. Fill out your menu to see how they compare. You can skip items and show the results at any time.
