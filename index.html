Non-Escalator Relationship Menu

Your partner has filled out their menu. Fill out yours to see how your answers compare. Their answers stay hidden until you've submitted yours.

This tool is a discussion aid for people in a relationship to identify and discuss their personal desires and relationship expectations. It's only the start of a conversation: revisit the menu when circumstances or relationships change.

  1. Each of you fills out the menu on your own.
  2. You each get a code. Send yours to the other person.
  3. Enter the other person's code to see where your answers match.

Nothing is stored on a server. Your answers live only in your code and in this browser. Anyone who has your code can see your answers, so only share it with your partner.
